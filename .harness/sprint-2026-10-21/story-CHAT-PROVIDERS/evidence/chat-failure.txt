About Anthropic

Claude models via the local claude CLI, non-interactive print mode. Runs on your Claude Pro or Max seat, not a metered API key.

Prompts are sent to Anthropic's servers.

Tool execution is disabled on every call (--tools ""): this connection answers chat, it does not run commands or touch files.
